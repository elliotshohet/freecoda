## Users
People seeking general conversation and coding help across browser, desktop, and terminal. Confirmed by the user during Impeccable initialization.

## Product Purpose
Freecoda is a conversational assistant and coding agent. Help users move from a question or idea to useful answers and reviewed project changes.

## Positioning
Free hosted inference uses the approved zero-price model catalog through Vercel AI Gateway. Preserve the free product promise, fair-use limits, and visible service availability; never silently substitute paid models.

## Capabilities and Constraints
General chat has no implicit repository access. Project work requires explicit folder trust and applicable write, command, tool, and skill permissions. Preserve account isolation, encrypted history, offline conflict handling, consent, and deletion semantics. Keep behavior consistent across clients through shared contracts and core agent logic.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Freecoda branding, the single-logo splash, and the signed-in user's real avatar with a graceful fallback. Refine the incumbent interface rather than replace its identity. Do not invent usage, release, performance, or customer claims.

## Evidence on Hand
README.md, root AGENTS.md, distribution documentation, and implemented source are the factual evidence. Hosted inference, updater installation, and release publication require independent live verification.

## Product Principles
- Make the current mode, access, and approval state clear.
- Keep conversation and project work connected without crossing trust boundaries.
- Preserve drafts, account privacy, and recoverable state.
- Report unavailable integrations honestly.

## Operating Context
Interactive terminal client with general chat and explicitly trusted project work, streamed answers, account history, slash commands, and approvals. Respect terminal dimensions, keyboard navigation, plain output, color capability, and terminal restoration. HTML/browser detector rules do not describe this surface.
